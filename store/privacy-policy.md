# Redraft privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-16.

Redraft is a Mac app by Santiago Alonso. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

**What Redraft handles.** When you press the Redraft shortcut, Redraft copies the text you selected and sends it, with your instruction, to the AI model you chose in Settings → Models.

- **Local model (LM Studio):** the text goes to LM Studio on your Mac or on a computer you set up. It doesn't leave your control.
- **Cloud provider (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek or fal):** the text goes directly from your Mac to that provider, using your own API key. Redraft asks for your permission the first time it sends text to each provider. That provider's privacy policy applies to the text it receives. If your instruction includes "use web search", OpenRouter also searches the web for it.

**What stays on your Mac.** API keys are stored in your Mac's Keychain. Results, the model used and any web sources are saved in Redraft's History inside the app's own storage on your Mac. Clearing History hides entries and keeps a recovery copy on your Mac.

**What Redraft never does.** It never sends your text or keys to the developer, never tracks you, never shows ads and never sells data. It reads text only when you call it.

**Permission.** Redraft asks for one macOS permission, listed under Privacy & Security → Accessibility, so it can press ⌘C and ⌘V for you when you call it.
